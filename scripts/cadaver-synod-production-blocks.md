12. Formosus was actually competent. A skilled diplomat and missionary, so popular that the people of Bulgaria specifically requested him as their archbishop.

13. That popularity was the problem. Pope John the Eighth saw him as a rival, accused him of coveting the papacy, and excommunicated him.

14. Formosus fled Rome, got himself un-excommunicated, worked his way back into favor, and in eight ninety-one, became pope anyway.

20. Arnulf marched on Italy, took Rome, and in eight ninety-six, Formosus crowned him emperor instead. For about five minutes, the plan worked.

36. If Stephen expected applause, he had badly misread the room. Even by ninth century standards, putting a corpse on trial struck Rome as too much.
