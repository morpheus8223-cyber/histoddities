## What was fixed and why

- **Typos removed:** "Hisoddities" and "Histodditie" (both were the channel's own name, misspelled, in the title).
- **Pipes/formatting removed:** `||`, `!!`, ALL CAPS words, emoji (👁️, 🤯), bracketed tags (`[Weird History]`), colon-prefix labels ("Weird History:", "Mass Hysteria:").
- **Channel name removed from titles:** "Histoddities" and "Hisoddities" no longer appear inside the title text itself.
- **Stale real-time reference removed:** "Olympics 2024" is now two years old and reads as dated; dropped entirely.
- **Every title reframed as a curiosity gap** (question, contradiction, or specific-detail hook) per the Weird History formula and, where a clean fit existed, the higher-performing History Matters question-actor structure (Olof Palme, Olympics).
- **Fact-check note:** two titles were deliberately hedged rather than overclaimed — "looks exactly like a lightbulb" (not "is a lightbulb," since the Dendera relief is disputed pseudo-archaeology) and "no one was ever convicted" for Palme (accurate — a suspect was named posthumously in 2020 but the case was closed without prosecution, so "unsolved" would overstate it).
